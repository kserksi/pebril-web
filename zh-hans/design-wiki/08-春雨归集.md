<a id="s-7-3"></a>

核心意象：「随风潜入夜，润物细无声」。照片像春雨一样逐滴归入局部汇聚点，逐渐凝聚成一个又一个图片集；时间轴像稳定的河道，在它们归位时缓慢显现。借用自然汇聚的节奏，不直接绘制雨滴、水纹或河流特效。

关键状态顺序

| 关键帧 | 主要变化 | 同步关系 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| K0 · Quiet Flow | 首屏底部半隐藏、错落的照片同向缓流并轻微浮动 | 时间轴不可见 |
| K1 · Full Reveal | 随用户下滑，照片逐渐完整浮现；完整显现后才进入归集 | 河流的自主微动平稳交出控制权 |
| K2 · Leading Photo | 各图片集的代表主图先下移并适度缩小 | 其他照片仍保留自然的过渡状态 |
| K3 · Second Follows | 主图与邻近图片形成约 20% 的遮盖感，第二张响应 | 后续图片按次序加入，不触发突跳 |
| K4 · Layered Gathering | 第三、第四、第五张陆续归集，逐渐出现克制的扇形层叠 | 后景弱化层开始显现，各集合允许轻微错峰 |
| K5 · Collection Forms | 最多五张清晰前景与少量弱化后景共同形成图片集预览 | 时间轴已经开始在固定位置显现 |
| K6 · Riverbed Reveal | 图片集趋于稳定、对齐各自时间坐标；时间刻度渐清晰 | 图片集归位与时间轴显现相互重叠、同步收束 |
| K7 · Stable Exploration | 第二屏完成，进入可触控、可键盘操作的稳定探索状态 | 动画退出主导，交互优先 |

上述 K0–K7 是六阶段叙事内部的分镜检查点，不新设八个独立播放章节，也不要求每个关键帧对应一段不重叠的滚动距离。K2–K4 的先后顺序与约 20% 的主图引导关系已确立；具体坐标、曲线、时间比例尚未冻结。

图片集的实际内容与可见预览

集合成员数与预览张数分离：图片集可包含多于五张照片；前景最多五张，以轻微扇形错位体现多图关系；实际少于五张时不复制照片凑数。

后景只表达深度：由少量柔和、低对比度或轻度模糊的层次暗示其余内容，而非把全部剩余照片逐张渲染；可以呈现真实数量信息，不以弱化卡片数量冒充实际总数。

自然运动不篡改真实归属：照片所属集合、可信拍摄时间与时间轴位置由真实数据语义决定。随机性只用于主图展示选择、微小视觉差异和可复现的运动编排，不应把空间相邻但数据无关联的照片误归为同一个集合。

Owner-authorized demo-data exception (2026-10-24): the owner explicitly permitted invented sample times and confirmed five collections of five photos from the supplied WebP pool. src/demo-photos.mjs persists five distinct demoTime values and fixed membership; the river and gathering must share this data. These values are fictional demonstration chronology, not EXIF/capture metadata, and must be identified as example times in the timeline. This exception does not authorize invented timestamps or inferred grouping for user-selected local photos.

滚动绑定：共享归一化滚动位置作为进度源，因此慢滚、快滚、暂停、反向滚动均对应同一组可恢复的空间状态；滚动速度影响进度推进快慢，不单独通过瞬时速度触发不可逆的排队动画。

轻量化优先：无需通过复杂实时模糊、为每个隐藏成员创建卡片或大量同时运行的图层来证明集合深度；应在性能和可访问性验证中决定后景层的实现细节。

## Owner-confirmed first prototype

The owner confirmed these prototype parameters:

- Keep the existing 1.3-viewport-height Prelude channel. Reveal the full river over another 0.5 viewport-height, then gather over 3 viewport-heights.
- Gathering 0–20% guides each leading photo to 20% horizontal projected overlap with its second member. Followers begin at 20%, 35%, 50% and 65%, approaching their fan arrangement by 80%; the last 20% settles the collections.
- Final card scale is approximately 75% of river size, with container-height limits. Fan angles remain within ±3°. The latest owner correction raises the fully revealed river to the upper scene after navigation has faded; all additional members share that same upper origin before descending.
- The timeline fades from gathering progress 30% and reaches full visibility with settlement. It preserves the persisted example-date intervals and supports horizontal browsing, instead of vertically staggering collections to fit dates.
Implementation details following the existing continuity, accessibility and interaction requirements:

- Reuse the currently flowing nodes; defer remaining source-pool members until gathering and give them the same upper-river geometry as the flowing members. Do not include those noncritical images in Prelude resource gating. Reversing to the river removes added nodes and restores current source identities and motion phase.
- Normal gathering is position-driven, pausable and reversible. Active exploration holds its settled composition through the final 20% when reversing slightly, then releases for a deliberate return. Reduced-motion mode presents static collection exploration rather than long scroll trajectories.

These are implemented prototype settings subject to visual review, not acceptance of the complete six-phase site. This paragraph describes the initial gathering-only iteration; current local-photo and closure implementation status is maintained in [the status topic](17-设计状态.md). Independent time-label filtering remains separate work.

仍待原型验证

约 20% 遮盖的实际计算口径（投影还是可见面积）、第三至第五张的具体启动间隔、各集合的错峰尺度、卡片的扇形角度、后景层的数量与模糊程度、归位路径、时间轴的细节显现区间及滚动区间长度，均为未冻结参数。

## Owner caption-container correction · 2026-10-25

Each collection's date/count now has its own bounded responsive caption inside the collection control. During formation, the caption gradually emerges upward from behind that stack; the photograph envelope, rather than a detached page heading, is its visual origin. The prototype uses gathering progress 65–100% for opacity and an offset relative to the caption's own height. It reaches its settled position above the stack and fades there during exit, without falling back into the photographs. Reverse scrolling restores the same caption state, and reduced motion uses the settled static placement. Date semantics, actual member counts and opening the full collection remain unchanged.

## Owner visible-first assembly correction · 2026-10-25

Gathering now captures the current visible river context when collection formation starts. Within each real collection, visible members are promoted into the preview, led by the photograph nearest the viewport center; remaining members follow in their existing source order. A visible member beyond the five-photo foreground limit may become the leader without dropping full membership. The central visible photograph also selects the initial collection/date for the ruler, so the sequence need not begin at the earliest sample date.

This is deterministic presentation ordering, not reassignment of photos, invented dates or new collection membership. The chosen ordering remains fixed during held/reversed gathering, and source nodes keep their actual identities. Opening the collection uses that same complete ordered member list so the enlarged photograph matches its current leader. Re-entering from the river may capture a new visible context after the source pool has continued flowing.
