## Owner motion/copy correction · 2026-10-25

The album's continuation cue is passive scroll guidance rather than a button or click-to-jump action. Its text and the separate Prelude explore cue float gently within stable hit areas; the Prelude action remains usable. Ripple feedback in the final scene does not insert visible explanatory prose into the legal band. Its status remains available to assistive technology. Reduced motion leaves the cues static.

Cue visibility correction (2026-10-25): the album continuation cue now runs its gentle motion throughout its actual visible interval, including near-complete Prelude positions. Requiring the intro to equal exactly 100% left the already-visible cue static; that exact-point gate is removed. It remains passive, keeps a stable hit area and respects reduced motion.
