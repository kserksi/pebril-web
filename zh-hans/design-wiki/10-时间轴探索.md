## 8.1 第二屏稳定状态

第二屏最终呈现完整的时间漫游演示。

其主要结构为：

顶部简洁的产品理念文案。

位于视觉中部、位置相对固定的时间轴。

位于时间轴上方的图片集。

必要的交互提示与本地照片演示入口。

第二屏不能只是一张动画结束后的静态截图。

它需要允许用户进行有限但真实的探索。

<a id="s-8-2"></a>

Owner-authorized sample chronology (2026-10-24): use the five persisted fictional demoTime values from src/demo-photos.mjs for the five approved sample collections, each containing five files. Clearly label their displayed times as examples. Do not regenerate dates on refresh or mistake them for original capture times. The real-time rules continue to apply to real/local-user photos. The data is implemented; gathering/timeline rendering and unresolved motion parameters are not yet accepted.

## 8.2 时间轴显现方式

时间轴以透明度、轻微对比度和结构细节逐渐显现。

不使用明显的飞入、弹跳或剧烈缩放。

时间轴在转场期间保持稳定视觉锚点。

图片集向时间轴对应位置移动，而不是让整条时间轴追逐图片集。

图片集归位与时间轴主体的淡入同时发生并逐渐收束；时间轴的线条、刻度可以分层补足，但不能等图片集全部完成才开始出现。两者在视觉上保持稳定的空间锚点与呼吸距离，不以碰撞、弹跳或明显的形态变换表现「承托」。

## 8.3 图片集与时间轴的关联反馈（v0.3 动效补充）

默认状态：图片集按其真实时间关系排列在时间轴上方，通过位置及刻度对齐建立联系；不绘制常驻连接线，避免增加视觉噪声。

聚焦状态：在鼠标悬停、键盘焦点进入、触控点击选中或其他明确聚焦操作时，当前图片集到对应时间位置之间可以轻柔淡入一条纤细的辅助连接线，并适度强调相应刻度；不得产生明显放大、弹跳或抢夺注意力的效果。

退出与可访问性：悬停结束或取消选中后，辅助线平稳淡出；触屏与键盘必须能获得等价反馈。时间关系应同时提供可读的时间信息，不能仅靠线条或颜色表达。

与滚动叙事协调：进入第二屏稳定交互状态后，关联线属于用户主动探索的反馈，不参与此前的春雨汇聚主动画；避免细微滚动打断当前聚焦操作。

<a id="s-8-4"></a>

Implemented prototype exploration (owner correction, 2026-10-25): a horizontal radio-style tuning ruler moves beneath a fixed central needle. Fine divisions, calendar-month marks, photo positions and the central readable date share one calendar-coordinate mapping; the later long-gap correction below supersedes globally uniform time-to-space scaling. The ruler itself accepts native touch panning and mouse dragging, including gestures beginning over collection controls; arrow keys tune continuously and Home/End reach its endpoints. Independent previous/next controls have been removed following the owner's second visual review. The first and last collections can both reach the needle. Unknown capture dates occupy an explicitly labeled separate collection, without fabricated time ticks. Sample dates remain labeled as fictional examples.

Owner update (2026-10-25): when horizontal input pauses or the pointer is released close to a collection node, the ruler gently aligns that node beneath the fixed needle. Attraction is limited to 36 CSS pixels or 8% of the viewport width, whichever is smaller, after 140 ms without further scrolling. Positions outside that range remain free; fresh input interrupts alignment, and reduced-motion mode aligns without an animated glide. This prototype tuning does not add separate navigation controls or capture vertical page scrolling.

The collection/ruler composition is raised toward mid-screen. Its vertical anchor clears the actual heading text and the full rotated-card envelope on short viewports rather than using one fixed percentage everywhere. The entire fixed navigation fades away for scene two. Collection buttons expose dates/counts, selectable states and focus/hover helper lines. Rear members progressively weaken behind their leading photograph. Small reverse movement within settlement preserves focused exploration; leaving settlement restores scroll-driven geometry. The browser-only photo experience sits directly below the tuner instead of being pinned near the bottom. The previous exact-coordinate interaction gate was a defect: normal scroll positions could disable both the ruler and file input. The prototype now includes a 0.6-viewport-height stable exploration interval, enables controls near completed settlement with CSS-pixel tolerance, and keeps them available across small initial exit movement. This distance is an implementation prototype value, not a newly owner-frozen design parameter. Visible selection controls and interaction hints appear only when they can actually receive input. Independent user-created time tags and OR filtering remain outside this prototype iteration.

Owner-requested collection viewing (2026-10-25): activating a settled collection photograph or its date/count opens a native modal viewer. Previous/next controls and Left/Right keys browse its complete, ordered membership, including members beyond the five-card foreground preview. Sample dates remain explicitly fictional, and local dates remain real or unknown. When an endpoint navigation button becomes disabled, focus moves to the available direction so keyboard browsing remains inside the viewer. Close/Escape returns focus to the collection, preserves the tuned ruler position and unlocks background scrolling; the modal holds background scrolling while open. Only the current image is decoded for viewing, with its original aspect ratio, a readable loading/failure status and cancellation of stale display results. The active timeline hit layer sits above its photograph previews so actual image clicks and drags reach the collection/ruler controls. This is a local preview, with no upload, persistent store or new dependency.

Owner-confirmed ruler changes (2026-10-25): long empty intervals between adjacent collections contract, with visible break marks rather than silently pretending that all intervals have the same scale. Each segment maps its real calendar interval continuously; exact collection-day nodes, minor/calendar ticks, photo leaders, panning and the needle readout use that same piecewise mapping. Author-provided calendar clocks remain available for spacing same-day sample/local collections, without shifting the displayed date through host-timezone conversion; source timestamps and local metadata are unchanged. Unknown dates remain separate and receive no invented calendar ticks.

A resumed regression exposed cumulative native scroll-pixel rounding while the detail scale changed. The controller now preserves one logical center date throughout generated scale frames, updates it for real input and clears it for direct node alignment. Generated frames do not count as fresh gesture samples. This preserves the previously required anchored readout rather than introducing another date scale.

The owner explicitly chose faster horizontal sliding to narrow the visible time range and enlarge date detail. It does not zoom out for broad traversal. The prototype smoothly varies horizontal detail between 1× and 2.5×, keeps the date beneath the fixed center needle anchored during scaling, and retains the chosen detail after the gesture; subsequent slower sliding returns toward the broader scale. Programmatic node alignment, keyboard endpoint commands and attraction do not generate a false speed signal. Existing free panning and bounded release attraction remain available. Reduce-motion mode applies the scale change directly. These numerical thresholds are prototype tuning, not frozen product parameters.

Viewer correction: the container fits the current image's true aspect ratio inside viewport limits, instead of retaining a large fixed-height panel. The header shows only the date or unknown-time value; close/previous/next use small inline SVG primitives with accessible names. A 440 ms native transform now carries the entire panel and its image from the selected collection's actual photo bounds, with restrained opacity and no bounce. Background blur begins with the opening and reaches 8 px; Close/Escape reverses the panel toward its live collection anchor over 260 ms while the blur and dimming recede. These later owner corrections supersede the earlier image-only opening. Photo changes keep the outgoing image until the next one is decoded, then use a 120 ms directional fade out and 220 ms entry; the elastic panel/frame dimensions follow the new ratio. Rapid requests retain the latest index. Reduced motion applies these changes without animation. Lifecycle cleanup closes immediately, cancels pending motion and releases the scroll lock. Resize cancels stale motion and refits the displayed or pending photo. All durations, offsets and blur strength remain prototype values. The heading example badge and visible selection-status badge are removed; truthful source labels and ruler instructions remain accessible.

Owner readout/context correction (2026-10-25): initial exploration centers on the collection of the current central visible river photograph, rather than forcing the first sample collection. The single readable time below the ruler is its fixed-needle readout. Repeated dates beneath collection nodes and month marks are removed; their correctly mapped tick geometry and compressed-gap marks remain. Collection dates/counts stay in the separate captions above the photos, and browsing retains the full real member list in the chosen visible-first order.

## 8.4 交互优先权

当用户开始与第二屏的时间轴、图片集或标签发生明确交互时，应优先保证交互稳定。

不应因为细微滚动或鼠标移动，使当前操作中的图片集突然重新进入转场动画。

具体处理方式，例如交互区域的稳定滚动区间、焦点转移及退出逻辑，后续在交互设计阶段确定。

## 8.5 时间标签基本规则

时间标签属于用户创建的独立标签体系。

时间标签的覆盖区间与照片原始拍摄时间、上传时间之间保持概念与数据上的独立。

选择时间标签不会改变照片的原始时间坐标。

多时间标签选择采用 OR 匹配：图片集只要匹配任一已选标签即可保持正常展示。

用户定义的标签颜色独立于品牌配色。

时间轴上的多标签重叠、Focused 标签及相关探索行为继续遵循此前确定的 Pebril 产品交互规范。
